## 3. Project Selected

### Smart Agriculture Assistant – AI-Based Crop Recommendation and Disease Detection System

Smart Agriculture Assistant is an AI-based agricultural support system designed to help farmers make better decisions about crop selection and plant health.

The system can analyze information such as soil properties, temperature, humidity, rainfall and other environmental conditions to recommend suitable crops. It can also use image-based AI techniques to identify possible crop diseases from plant leaf images.

The proposed system combines:

* Machine Learning
* Crop recommendation
* Image classification
* Plant disease detection
* Data analysis
* AI-based decision support

The main goal is to provide farmers with useful recommendations and early disease identification through an easy-to-use digital platform.

The refined prompt produced more detailed, structured and relevant content. The same iterative process was applied to all communication artifacts.

# 7. Literature Review

## Purpose

A literature review was prepared to understand existing research related to machine learning in agriculture, crop recommendation and plant disease detection.

### Initial Prompt

```text
Give a literature review about AI in smart agriculture.
```

### Problems

The initial response discussed AI in agriculture broadly but did not identify specific research areas related to the proposed project.

### Improved Prompt

```text
Prepare a short literature review related to machine learning-based
crop recommendation, agricultural decision support systems and
deep learning-based plant disease detection. Discuss their benefits
and limitations and identify a research gap relevant to a Smart
Agriculture Assistant.
```

### Final Review

Machine learning has been increasingly used in agriculture to analyze environmental and soil data and support crop selection. Crop recommendation models can use parameters such as temperature, rainfall, humidity and soil nutrients to identify potentially suitable crops.

Deep learning and image classification techniques have also been applied to plant disease detection. These methods can analyze leaf images and classify visible disease patterns.

However, crop recommendation and disease detection are often implemented as separate applications. Crop recommendation systems focus mainly on environmental and soil conditions, while disease detection systems focus on plant images.

### Research Gap

A potential research gap is the development of an integrated platform that combines crop recommendation and plant disease detection with a user-friendly interface and personalized agricultural assistance.

# 14. Ethical Considerations

AI-generated technical content should be used responsibly.

Important considerations include:

* Verify AI-generated technical information before submission.
* Do not generate or present fake experimental results.
* Do not use fabricated research references.
* Check generated content for plagiarism.
* Protect farmers' personal and agricultural data.
* Clearly distinguish between proposed and implemented features.
* AI predictions should be treated as decision support rather than guaranteed agricultural advice.
* Human expertise should be considered for important agricultural decisions.
